# Analytical illustrations

Figures 3, 5, and 10 are schematic illustrations, not outputs of Monte Carlo experiments.
Their standalone TikZ sources are `analytic-filter-support.tex`, `ridge-filter-contours.tex`,
and `two-phase-backward-path.tex`, respectively. Compile with a TeX distribution containing
TikZ, standalone, amsmath, amssymb, and hyperref, for example:

```sh
pdflatex analytic-filter-support.tex
```

The hand-drawn paths and real-axis marks are illustrative and do not encode sampled observations.
